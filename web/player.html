UNDERCROFT

Original NES roguelite. Five floors down, then out.

Loading the emulator…

A shoots. Hold it to keep your aim while you move, so you can strafe. B drops a bomb, which also breaks rocks. Start opens the map.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD to move, X or K for A, Z or J for B, Enter for Start.

This page runs the same ROM file as dist/undercroft.nes in the repository.
